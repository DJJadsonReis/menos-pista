The verdict

At a glance, you know if it pays off.

Menos Pista compares each offer with the per-distance and per-hour targets you set and with your vehicle cost. On the detail screen, the Analysis explains why in short lines.

  • ACCEPT

    Meets your per-distance and per-hour targets.

  • CONSIDER

    Close to the limit. Check the destination and time of day.

  • DECLINE

    Below what you set: too much driving for too little pay.

The app only shows the analysis. It never taps the screen and never accepts or declines rides for you.

Features

Everything the app does for you.

Verdict pop-up

As soon as the offer appears, a card shows the amount, earnings per distance and per hour, profit % and rating, with ACCEPT, CONSIDER or DECLINE.

Estimated profit

Estimates profit with your vehicle cost per distance (fuel, maintenance, tires and depreciation), set in Settings > Vehicles.

Pickup and trip, separately

Shows distance and time to the passenger and for the trip, plus the total distance and time of the ride.

History and finances

Keep analyzed offers, filter by Today, 7 days or 30 days, see averages per distance and per hour, log income and expenses and track daily, weekly and monthly goals.

On-device reading

Reads the offer through Accessibility and, when needed, from a screenshot with OCR on the phone itself. It does not read passwords or messages.

Shifts and battery saver

Mark the start and end of your shift to measure your day. With battery saver on, the app reads less often to use less power.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Menos Pista free?

Yes. The essential tools are free, with no required subscription. The app is funded by Google AdMob ads, which never show while an offer is on screen. A Pro plan (monthly or yearly) shows fewer ads and unlocks Pro features.

Does it work in my country?

Today, automatic offer reading works only with the Brazilian versions of rideshare and delivery apps (screens in Portuguese, prices in BRL). Support for more countries and languages is coming soon. The free ride calculator on this site works anywhere, in any currency, in miles or kilometers.

Which apps does it read?

In Brazil: Uber, inDrive, Rappi, Lalamove, Loggi, Cabify and other ride and delivery apps. You choose which ones to monitor in the app settings.

Why does the app need Accessibility access?

That is how it reads the offers on the screen of ride and delivery apps and shows whether they are worth it. Reading happens on your phone, and the app does not read passwords or messages. See the Privacy policy for details.

Does the app accept or decline rides for me?

No. It only shows the analysis on a card. Accepting or declining is always your decision, in the platform app.

Will it work on my phone?

It works on Android 8.0 or newer. On Android 13 or newer you need to allow restricted settings, as explained on the download page.

I need help. How do I contact you?

Open a ticket in the app (Settings) or on the Support page.
