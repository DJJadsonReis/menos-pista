How it is calculated

  • Total distance = pickup distance + trip distance.
  • Earnings per mile/km = amount ÷ total distance. Earnings per hour = amount ÷ (minutes ÷ 60).
  • Cost = cost per mile/km × total distance. Profit = amount − cost. Profit per hour = profit ÷ (minutes ÷ 60).

The Menos Pista app does this math automatically when an offer shows up. For now, automatic offer reading works only with apps in Brazil (Portuguese screens, prices in BRL); more countries are coming soon. This calculator works anywhere.
