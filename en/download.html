How to install, step by step

1Allow the install

Open the downloaded APK (from the browser notification or the Files app). If you see "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source", tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source. Then go back.

2Install the APK

Tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details and Install anyway. When it finishes, tap Open.

3Allow restricted settings (Android 13 or newer)

Apps installed outside the store come with Accessibility blocked. To unlock it:

  1. Try to turn on Menos Pista in Accessibility once. You will see "Restricted setting": tap OK.
  2. Open Settings > Apps > Menos Pista.
  3. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) at the top and Allow restricted settings.
  4. Confirm with your PIN, pattern or fingerprint.

If the three-dot menu does not show up, you have not tried to turn on Accessibility yet: do item 1 first.

4Turn on the Accessibility service

Go to Settings > Accessibility > Installed apps (or "Downloaded services") > Menos Pista and turn it on. This is how the app reads offers on screen and shows the "worth it?" card. Reading happens on your phone, and the app never accepts or declines rides for you.

5Allow the other permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications: for offer alerts, "reading stopped" and the end-of-day reminder.
  • Unrestricted battery (ignore battery optimization): so Android does not close the Copilot while you work.

Then follow the app's welcome steps: choose the apps you use and add your vehicle for the profit calculation.

How to check the file (optional)

The SHA-256 code is the APK's "fingerprint". The app checks it on every update; if the file is different, it does not install.

Reading stopped?

If Accessibility turns itself off, repeat steps 3 and 4 (the app shows the same guide in Settings > Permissions). On some phones it also helps to set Menos Pista battery to unrestricted in Settings > Apps > Menos Pista > Battery.
