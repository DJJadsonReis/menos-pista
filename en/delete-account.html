Delete your Menos Pista account

You can delete your account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Menos Pista.
  2. Go to Settings > Account and sync.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm with your Google account.

Without the app

Send a direct message to @grupojadson on Instagram, or an email to grupojadson@gmail.com, with the text "Delete Menos Pista account" and the Google email used in the app. Deletion is done within 30 days and you receive a confirmation.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, sign-up date, last use, app version, apps used, plan and choice about receiving news).
  • Analyzed rides and Finances entries copied to your account.
  • Notification tokens of your devices.
  • Your sign-in (Firebase Authentication user).

What may be kept

  • Payment records of direct sales, for the period required by law (tax obligations).
  • Data saved only on your phone (local history and screenshots) stays on the device until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Data controller

Grupo Jadson (contact above). See also the Privacy policy.
